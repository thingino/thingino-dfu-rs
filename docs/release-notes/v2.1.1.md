thingino-dfu 2.1.1 is a fix and memory release for `dfu-remote` on an ESP32, the thingino
dev backpack's daemon (thingino/thingino-backpack). The CLI, the web flasher, Android and
a Linux daemon behave as before, and the wire format is unchanged.

## The fix

After a bootstrap through the ESP32 daemon, the DFU gadget could come up seven seconds
late, and once not at all. It happened when a listing reached the camera just as its
bootrom handed over to U-Boot: the listing's detection kept retrying the departed device
for 6.5 s, holding it open, and ESP-IDF enumerates the next device on the port only once
every handle on the old one is closed. The ESP-IDF backend now closes a device as soon
as it sees that it has left, and the gadget comes up within a second.

## More memory, faster transfers

On an ESP32-S3 without PSRAM, the lowest free memory during a bootstrap rises from 57 KB
to about 100 KB, and during reads and writes from about 110 KB to about 120 KB:

- The daemon holds a streamed payload 4 KiB at a time, and on ESP-IDF reads its socket 4
  KiB at a time: the TCP window there is smaller than the 16 KiB buffers were.
- The ESP-IDF backend sends bulk transfers 4 KiB at a time.
- The padded copy of the stage-1 image is freed before U-Boot goes out.

The ESP-IDF backend also keeps its control transfer between requests, where a 16 MiB
write used to allocate a DMA buffer for every 4 KiB block. Through the dev backpack, 16
MiB reads are 4% faster and writes with verify 2% faster.

Its tasks now carry their names (`usb-lib`, `usb-client` and `usb-watch`) in a FreeRTOS
task list, where they all read `pthread` before.

## Verified

- A T31X through the ESP32-S3 daemon over Wi-Fi, without PSRAM, in four runs against
  three on 2.1.0: bootstrap 5.4 s (5.6 s), 16 MiB read 65.9 s (68.5 s), 16 MiB write with
  verify 143.9 s (146.5 s), every verify passing.
- Four bootstraps with a listing timed into the handover: the gadget came up within 0.9 s
  each time.
- The full gate: 1303 tests, clippy for the host, the browser build and the ESP32-S3, and
  the page's tests.
